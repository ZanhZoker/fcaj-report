# FCAJ x Agentic AI Build Week

**Date:** 25 July 2026  
**Format:** In person, Ho Chi Minh City  
**Role:** Attendee

“Show Up. Build. Pitch. WIN!” presented how teams turned focused problems into
demonstrable MVPs with Agentic AI patterns and AWS services. Speakers and teams
included Joseph Marazota, Nguyễn Gia Hưng, One Team, Signal Scout, Team Plan,
Team 3K, and Six Pillars.

#### Highlights

- Start with a specific user pain point and one testable core workflow.
- Multi-agent designs help when responsibilities can be separated clearly; for
  simple work they can add unnecessary latency, cost, and debugging effort.
- Human approval and traceability are essential for high-risk actions.
- Observability, security, cost, and scope control belong in the MVP.

#### Key takeaways and application

The event reinforced the current pipeline boundary: clean interaction data,
preserve IDs, publish an auditable quality report, and hand the result to the ML
role. A future assistant could summarize quality reports or help draft Athena
queries, but data changes, deletion, and deployment should remain human-approved
actions.

#### Personal reflection

Watching teams solve very different problems with a common platform showed that
product value comes from understanding users and controlling scope, not from
architecture complexity alone. The pitches also demonstrated the value of a
clear sequence: problem, workflow, architecture, evidence, and outcome.
